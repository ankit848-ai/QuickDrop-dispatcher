How it works

  1. An order arrives. The event heap hands the simulator the next event in time order.
  2. Pick a rider. Free riders go into a min-heap keyed by Manhattan distance to the restaurant; the top is the nearest.
  3. No one free? The order joins a priority queue: VIP first, then oldest.
  4. Route it. BFS finds the shortest path around blocked cells: rider → restaurant → customer.
  5. Delivery done. The rider becomes free at the customer and takes the top waiting order.
